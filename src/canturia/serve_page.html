How Canturía works

About a topic

Type a theme or a sentence. Canturía asks the chosen lyricist to write the lyrics, then sends them to ACE-Step with your genre, length, and English vocals. The length is honoured exactly.

Genre and Options

Genre chips are presets; you can also type your own genre. A genre is always required. Options are tweaks to that genre — the summary at the top of Options shows the final style prompt and the parameters that will be sent.

Instrumental skips lyrics. Vocal language, BPM, key, time signature, seed, model, steps, and guidance are sent to ACE-Step for this request. LoRA is server-side: load an adapter path, then use, bypass, scale, or unload it.

Preview lyrics

Write the words first, without generating audio. Edit them, then press Sing these lyrics to send them to ACE-Step. This is the easiest way to compare lyricists before committing to a song.

Lyricists

Ollama runs locally. OpenAI-compatible is bring-your-own-key: OpenAI, LM Studio, llama.cpp, OpenRouter, and other /v1/chat/completions endpoints. Keys live in ~/.canturia.yaml and are only sent to the endpoint you configure. ACE-Step LM is experimental and often returns [Instrumental]; use it for comparison, not as the default.

Write lyrics

Paste your own words. [Verse] and [Chorus] tags help the singer. Your lyrics go straight to ACE-Step; length is honoured exactly.

Surprise me

ACE-Step invents the lyrics itself from your words. Canturía adds “with lyrics in English” to the prompt, because that is the only lever that moved the language in testing. Even then, length and language are best-effort, and the result may wander. Use it when you want a happy accident.

Nothing leaves your box

Ollama runs locally; ACE-Step is the server you configured. There is no account, no telemetry, and no hosted service.

Saving

The session gallery is in memory. Save writes the MP3 and a JSON sidecar to ~/Music/Canturia (or the server's configured folder).

First generation

The first cold ACE-Step request can take minutes while model weights load. Later requests are much faster.
