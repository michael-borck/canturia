How Canturía works

About a topic

Type a theme or a sentence. Canturía asks Ollama on this machine to write the lyrics, then sends them to ACE-Step with your genre, length, and English vocals. The length is honoured exactly.

Write lyrics

Paste your own words. [Verse] and [Chorus] tags help the singer. Your lyrics go straight to ACE-Step; length is honoured exactly.

Surprise me

ACE-Step invents the lyrics itself from your words. Canturía adds “with lyrics in English” to the prompt, because that is the only lever that moved the language in testing. Even then, length and language are best-effort, and the result may wander. Use it when you want a happy accident.

Nothing leaves your box

Ollama runs locally; ACE-Step is the server you configured. There is no account, no telemetry, and no hosted service.

Saving

The session gallery is in memory. Save writes the MP3 and a JSON sidecar to ~/Music/Canturia (or the server's configured folder).

First generation

The first cold ACE-Step request can take minutes while model weights load. Later requests are much faster.
